QuPath
------

training, feature configuration and viewer integration adapt or refer
to the sources below. The extension implementations were adapted for
QuPath 0.7.0 and modified on 2026-11-25. The upstream source files
remain available under GPL-3.0-or-later.

feature selection, held-out validation, Random Trees feature importance,
the comparison divider and the pixel calibration API.
